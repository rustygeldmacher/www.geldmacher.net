Season 6 has four beet-based recipes, the first two I did in March, and the second two I did
over the last week.

I first set out to get some {% good_eats_recipe_link Pickled Beets %} going in the fridge. Like
many recipes, I decided I'd halve this one. I began by getting 3 medium beets prepared for
roasting.

![roast beets ingredients](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczMo0FnDFEJ6L3_sajARJRA-KWawBWaDO51Inbkag4pRBQfB2iBSleYcrpAj37jDp7ym2lfqQrXs_chwq_FtAhEuAsfUq2tvqjfn629OKWxo4Q385mtoAgro0yD1bDk9B6-AbV2bek79Cp87_UAFucPu=w2250-h1688-s-no-gm?w=500)

I had forgotten to grab fresh rosemary, so I used dried. I don't think that made much difference in
the end. While the beets were roasting, I cut up some red onion.

![cut up onions](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczP6gS7q82jwg31_ru3if1rv2__U7ql4Jx2jfvjtioXpM7xIm-uA765LOSrfqQR393psUVEm450t2l3-BpTsBBoIkTqQ88ogh6Dpwu-5rcbSYFsvWdQlnZ_G3U6y1GrPsheUn8L_P1a7OM_pDXYQ-XBS=w2250-h1688-s-no-gm?w=500)

Then prepared the picking liquid.

![pickling liquid ingredients](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczP3SnRpUEwYDF31TdGTVUqp6lURUPfy1wtPrDuGe5pNieEFYanBCaS7lNGsiSSOENBfKktjw90apXrmotgOkqGbuuxuyUigGV62jHfOOVV6DHvG_ly5CutMtLooHeH6UeygY3HgUxkmw1l7yZQ2O06x=w2250-h1688-s-no-gm?w=500)

When the beets were finished roasting, I peeled and sliced them, then stacked them with the onions
in a quart mason jar.

![beets pickling](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczM1qKNq1vFuwhatSSMH3lWkMEtt_k0Hkkxf4QVH64e5163iucPYiNNLcoYyzMU7lUenYj5X8Fdc9dQSI4SpLTTCZ85jJxuczO2CmsdWsphxFJpiMep-En7PWGuCMHOP4WNyWIAKdjbxHnV5qyfjD96o=w1266-h1688-s-no-gm?w=500)

I used almost all of the beets, but there was maybe a half beet left over. Also, half of the total
pickling liquid didn't quite cover it all, so I boiled another half cup (quarter cup of vinegar plus
quarter cup of water) -- no extra sugar -- and that got me to the top. I then put the jar into the
fridge for the next 7 days.

Meanwhile, since I'd went and bought so many beets, I used the rest of them to prepare some
{% good_eats_recipe_link Beet Slaw %}. Again, I halved this recipe too, because it seemed like
it made _a lot_ of slaw.

The recipe says to boil the beets, but that seemed weird to me and [Allison](https://allisoncooksgoodeats.com/2017/05/25/episode-80-salad-daze-ii-the-long-arm-of-the-slaw/)
steamed them instead, so that's what I did.

![steaming beets](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczOCbqRPdmO1bNjD_Y8NDX5nrBE7LODRX8RNR31Kv1TR6vwQm1VxevolKWWZ-eIArH1AYacyVcRybSm05JTBO6tRERxrvEj9SsvTsywmsNK_3byoj-PHD37ocMwEhcDkPyXOiLVU8pXyh6UEHgDcbXXh=w748-h579-s-no-gm?w=500)

It took about 35 minutes to steam the beets to where they seemed cooked through. Once they were done,
peeled, and cooled I put them through the [little spiralizer](https://www.amazon.com/OXO-Grips-Handheld-Spiralizer-Green/dp/B013KAW5IQ)
that I'd bought. We've had the big contraption ones in the past, but we rarely used it and it took up
so much space that it wasn't worth keeping when we moved. But this little one takes up very little
space and is worth to keep around.

![spiralized beets](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczNA_vhKGdKyRLeIGRcdnq4iUe0iJZ1VSW-xoFIyGcaACntdfp97OmdSDZ0qULtml_fjWgb9O0VzN5ISoqgx8r7GU_7tEGPfJfdjWY_FWFYeFCnvupmEac9pIInEuPoeDFx-kdqxa_3BDHxtUNuzHaYb=w836-h627-s-no-gm?w=500)

I put the beet spirals in a colander (they didn't really drain all that much) while I sliced the
remaining ingredients with a mandolin and put them right on top.

![rest of the veggies on top](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczOwQ1ZaoXc79tHwJUCQhb9fbmGC7Ua2RqNgOQoz5F5RTY6YZz1gl0OZi4Tmk7i1QOEU4MfX1d8qR9Wy0Pft1xYYvINf4_UiOBWgo1uudbBCIJyngliwRY66U_9Ka-8oWgZGgz2I8YUt2hR8J4KbdcMf=w2250-h1688-s-no-gm?w=500)

Then I mixed up the dressing.

![slaw dressing](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczMp2YChMbVOwrYKhMlwDPViNN5AlaJHTV8kvrR8LAwlJ6uJA2i911_Ld2AZZojRLLR1HtuNTfOaZhd8y4Fr_TM0SO-ZmXqy7mK4bcjKpanx9Fq7TnQ_f_3jC_gy-o3X6ijQ4cI-8luAGumUEZ_XEcZl=w1842-h1688-s-no-gm?w=500)

And finally, put it all together with some goat cheese mixed in.

![beet slaw](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczMdKbMO1vBbIzfEHVVBy96fhEUTOdxjibQ1qwHjxrJwT4Ts1Aw-l52ETCDTmwEu0E6FqWdneKsMCLfPEjLyyH44emrXSwZ1UZQxjp4H9MGsCybSyPtu1XfuiXbenN-4Oo6aZ1mjhYqRLHvjLDty8cDu=w2428-h1688-s-no-gm?w=500)

I made this on Sunday, and let it sit overnight in the fridge. On Monday, we gave it a try. It
was definitely beet-y, but we really didn't enjoy it all that much. I think the word that went
around was: pointless. It's a fine example of a slaw as per the episode but we were not sure
what going through all the trouble bought us here. Some spiralized beets with a little vinaigrette
would go just as far if not further. The flavor wasn't bad, it's just not really something we'd
ever want, or would make again.

Come Friday, the pickled beets were ready to try. I popped them open, put some in a bowl and
then cut the slices up. We really did want to like these. However, after the initial tang of
the pickling, it was a little too strong and just kind of strange tasting. I don't think I'd
make these again.

Unfortunately, both of these beet recipes left us wanting. On a side note, I wish plain old
roasted beets were included as a recipe in this season -- when I roasted the beets for the slaw,
we voraciously ate the leftovers and would have given them a 4 or so. But the two applications
of beets this time around just weren't worth the trouble.
