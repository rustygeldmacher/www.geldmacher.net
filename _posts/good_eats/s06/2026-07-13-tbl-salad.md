Tomatoes continue to be in season so I thought that {% good_eats_recipe_link TBL Panzanella %}
would be a good side dish for some burgers.

Since doing stuff gluten free makes everything harder, I started the day before by baking a
fresh gluten free sandwich loaf.

![sandwich loaf](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczOCSNE0v0FLhYX4m32Ym5W5RxeuS24GKX-PWHckDN76xQSVmLhK8WtKVfSXbPQXrCp6bpeyQ1OZ9g7pC28MX4ON5Y_g63JafRb2rTCpZWHxVQ7M5phAg8fkbyVh60ERrwp0RxcPvFsz_nQ_fcnmcsq9=w2658-h1682-s-no-gm?w=500)

I then cut it up and left it overnight to dry out, which if you're familiar with gluten free
bread, is not a difficult ask.

![bread chunks](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczM8-sB7kGnO6ySpcZhJ8hQv-HjD0WEg8flx86dwCm0mJBvUF9fPBhCMQ1zMd1Emv8PC3aY0Ox27kIUZVYw7Pohb-D_xwX_ip_xGtKRxQ77iK6_1_IVRbAW6HV_Qn38LuAQ1kQ0TDrl1xOZQULHSu1fT=w2244-h1682-s-no-gm?w=500)

The next day I cooked up some bacon, made the dressing, and sauteed up a pint of grape tomatoes.

![grape tomatoes sauteing](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczMX72f3ook5jL9-jf448vEBjU475JFSxjcaLQYVLkycvmCggwBaB16IgCt3V4yh_9hhy78pVXx6jJvTbRwQW8rxtDZwX5h6ZsecCJRhNrrbnA-4xV7uSxQQX7cIBuCN4tsQ-j1ykqA5il4j3Hi7XfGN=w1262-h1682-s-no-gm?w=400)

After everything was prepped, I tossed the bread with the bacon grease, washed the lettuce,
crushed up the bacon, and cut up some basil.

![all the ingredients](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczP-RjG9MN3jlPaUAF2qmV5fuPp9vvGdb-IHpcQxUaLChCNQuMHr0HLijnzykxnuSdkTdovbhSfTQDawyqEztaPjMYCAChSrY4LsdvpDKh6z0oaEBYPyEbAklUzf1yilNaZO-ufgZMh96KD9IhblDfU_=w2242-h1682-s-no-gm?w=500)

The recipe calls for basil and mint, but we love basil around here so I skipped the mint and
just doubled up on basil.

![salads](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/pw/AP1GczPckV2deaEDmaG_FiGF0ERc9ZUdkOBtH4YZbkXaKHdsL6q5nxiwC4-1DTfMQzBcGX67yyoAh_Yk6SizzyNMGqiisEveV_2WB_0KqtXsOSAmvJxQTlQBPTXl7VEfaxDG55QHno9P_QO_AQMfz3eaUXP-=w1262-h1682-s-no-gm?w=400)

Once everything was ready, I tossed it all together and plated the salads. I really love the
bacon+tomato flavor of a BLT and this delivered. I also really liked how the bread soaked up
the bacon grease and the dressing, making for a soft and flavorful crouton. The rest of the
family did not like this as much, though. They'd have given this a 2 versus my 4, so I settled
on a three. Probably won't be making this again, though.
